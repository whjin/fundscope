**About FundScope:**
FundScope is a SaaS tool for retail investors who want to understand what's inside their Chinese mutual funds. It aggregates fund holdings data from public sources, visualizes top-10 stock positions, sector exposure, and market-cap distribution, and overlays real-time stock quotes — helping investors make more informed decisions.

I built FundScope because I personally struggled with the same problem: Chinese fund apps show performance charts but hide the actual stock holdings behind layers of menus. I wanted a fast, clean, no-login-required dashboard that answers "what stocks does this fund actually own?" in seconds. The product currently offers a free tier with basic analysis and a **$9.99/month Pro subscription** with unlimited fund queries, holding-change tracking, and priority support.

业务详情：
**Product:** FundScope — a web-based fund holdings analysis dashboard for retail investors who hold Chinese mutual funds. It turns opaque fund reports into clear, interactive visualizations in seconds.

**Key features:**

- Instant fund search with fuzzy matching over a built-in fund list
- Top-10 holdings table: stock code, name, day change %, holding ratio, market value, and position changes
- ECharts donut chart for holding-ratio distribution and bar chart for market-cap ranking
- Real-time stock quotes overlaid on holdings (Tencent/Sina feed)
- Fund basics: latest NAV, day change, 1-year return, fund manager
- No login required for free tier; works on mobile and desktop

**Pricing:**

- **Free tier:** basic fund search and holdings table, limited queries per day
- **Pro tier: $9.99/month** — unlimited fund queries, holding-change tracking, data export, priority support. 7-day free trial, cancel anytime.

**Target customers:** Individual retail investors in China (and overseas Chinese investors) who buy mutual funds and want to understand the underlying stock holdings before deciding to buy, hold, or sell. Not for institutions or professional traders.
